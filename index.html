01 / Evidence

The voice is already on the page

Six patterns pulled straight from nightfallhealth.com. Everything below grows out of these, so the brand book stays a description of the brand rather than a wish list.

Sentences in pairs

A short claim, then the line that completes it. This is the signature rhythm.

Insight when you can fix it. Care when you can't.

The gap, framed as a gap

The problem sits with the information, and the reader keeps their dignity.

Data isn't guidance. Nightfall turns data into action.

Real people as the proof

The word real appears three times across the page. Treat it as a promise, since it reads as one.

A real, board-certified sleep physician, one appointment away.

Every block ends in a verb

Connect, see, know what to change. Schedule, meet, get support. The reader always leaves with an action.

Numbers carry units

7h 42m. 9 min. 91%. Precision does the persuading, so superlatives can stay out.

The archetype is stated in the footer

The last line of the site says it in five words.

Designed with care for your sleep.

03 / Reader

Three states, three tones

The same person moves between these. Write for the state, then pick the hook family that matches it.

State one

Confused

Has worn an Oura or a Whoop for a year. Sees a score every morning and has nothing to do with it. Healthy, and stuck. This is the core audience.

Needs clarity. Lead with hook family F2.

State two

Tired

Sleeps seven hours and wakes up wrecked. Has tried supplements and routines. Starting to believe something is wrong with them.

Needs hope. Lead with F1, then F3.

State three

Worried

Suspects apnea or real insomnia. Knows a doctor is the answer, and the next opening is four months out, in a building far away.

Needs access. Lead with F5.

04 / Promise

One promise, three ways to say it

We tell you what is really happening in your sleep, and we stay with you until it is fixed. The central promise. Everything else is a version of this.
  • Your data means something. We tell you what.
  • One thing to change, in place of a checklist.
  • A real doctor the moment the app reaches its limit.
05 / Tone

Seven rules, with the line that breaks each one

Rule seven is the one that slips most often. The sleep category runs on fear of death and dementia. Nightfall stays out of that room, because a caregiver who frightens people to sell has stopped being one.

06 / Lexicon

Words that belong to us

The word intelligence describes the product. Keep it away from the reader, since "get smarter about sleep" turns a customer into a student.

Brand words
Words we leave to other brands
Phrasings to skip
  • Any sentence that opens with the word Stop.
  • Any mortality statistic used as a threat.
  • Any promise of a cure.
  • Anything shaped like "doctors hate this".
07 / Craft

How the sentences are built

Length

Under 15 words on average

The site already writes this way. Keep it there.

Person

We and you

The brand is we. The reader is you. Every time.

Signature

The pair

A short claim, then its consequence. Use it in openings and closings, and let the middle breathe.

Punctuation

Two sentences beat one long one

A full stop does the work that a long pause was going to do.

Numbers

Units and context

"9 minutes to fall asleep" says something. A bare 9 says nothing.

Endings

Close on a verb

Something to do, in place of something to feel. Caps stay in UI labels only.

11 / Organic

Three formats on repeat

Repetition is how a voice becomes recognisable. Three shapes, posted often, beat ten shapes posted once.

Format one

The number that surprises

One figure from sleep science, explained in three sentences, closing on a single practical conclusion.

Format two

The morning question

Something the reader feels and struggles to name. It ends on the explanation, and the product waits.

Format three

The correction

A common belief about sleep that turns out to be wrong. Calm tone, since a caregiver never makes anyone feel small.

Formats we skip
  • Lists shaped like "7 hacks".
  • Screenshots of a personal score with the explanation missing.
  • Anything that opens with "Most people don't realize".

13 / Open

Three calls that belong to the team

01

The Caregiver to Sage ratio

This book sets it at 70 to 30. Moving science to the front changes the whole lexicon, so decide it once.

02

Medical risk

Fear stays out here. Apnea may deserve a calm exception, said plainly and once.

03

The word alpha
